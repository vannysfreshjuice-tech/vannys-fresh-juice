# Vanny's Fresh Juice user manual

Last updated: 16 November 2026 (day list, money paid ahead)

This app keeps the whole juice business in one place: the fruit you buy, the juice you make, what is in the fridge, what you sell and deliver, what customers owe, reminders, expenses, staff and profit. It works on a phone like an app and on a laptop in the browser.

> **The records are kept in the cloud.** Every phone and laptop sees the same records, and each person opens the app with their own PIN. The app also works with no signal: what you record is sent when the connection returns. Still download a backup every week (Settings > Backup): the free cloud plan keeps no backups of its own.

## Putting it on your phone

**iPhone**

1. Open the app's address in **Safari** (not Chrome).
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

**Android**

1. Open the app's address in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **three dots** at the top right.
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**), then **Add**.

The Vanny's icon now sits on your Home Screen and opens full screen like any other app, even with no signal.

> On a laptop, open the same address in Chrome or Edge and bookmark it.

## Getting started

The first time, Home shows **Getting started**:

Before this, the owner sets up her PIN (see **Opening the app**).

1. Tap **Add the usual juices, fruit and bottles**. This adds 5 single-fruit juices and 7 juice cocktails with their recipes, the fruit and ingredients, and the bottles, caps and labels. Change any of them later on **Recipes and prices** and **Stock**.
2. Tap **Add the staff** and add everyone who works at Vanny's, the owner too (see **Staff and wages**).
3. Give each person who will use the app their own **PIN** (Staff > the person; see **Opening the app**).
4. Check the bottle sizes and prices in **Settings**.
5. Write each day's juice on the **Day list** (who took juice and whether they paid), or sell at the counter on **Sell**.
6. When you are ready, record what you buy (**Buying**) and each batch you make (**Production**): the app then knows what is in the fridge and what each juice really costs. Until then you can still sell (see **Settings**, Lists and freshness).

## Opening the app

Each person has their own PIN of 6 digits. It opens their part of the app (see **People and what they can open**) on any phone or laptop. The app locks itself after 5 minutes away.

**The owner, the very first time:** open the app, tap **The owner**, then **First time? Set up the owner's PIN**. Type 6 digits twice and tap **Save the PIN**. Choose digits that are hard to guess: the app refuses 111111, 123456 and the like. This is done once; on another phone, tap **The owner** and type the PIN.

**Giving a person their PIN (owner):** in **Staff**, open the person. Under **their sign-in**, type their phone number and a PIN for them, then tap **Give a PIN**. Tell them the PIN yourself. This needs the internet.

**Staff, the first time on a phone:** open the app, tap **I work at Vanny's**, type your phone number (the one the owner used), then your PIN. The first time on a phone needs the internet; after that the PIN alone opens the app, even with no signal.

**Opening the app:** type your PIN on the pad (on a laptop the number keys work too). After 5 wrong tries the app makes you wait, a little longer each time.

**Locking and changing person:** tap your name with the lock at the top of the screen (on a laptop, at the bottom of the menu). On the PIN pad, **Someone else** lets the next person sign in with their own details (this needs the internet).

**Changing your PIN:** Settings > **Your PIN** > **Change my PIN** (your present PIN, then the new one twice). Your other phones then ask for the new PIN. Settings is for the owner only: staff who want a different PIN ask the owner to give them a new one.

**Forgot the PIN?** Tap **Forgot the PIN?** on the PIN pad. Owner: a link is emailed to your email address; open it, type a new PIN of 6 digits as the new password, then use the new PIN. Staff: ask the owner to open your name in Staff and tap **Give a new PIN**. The records are never lost.

**Someone leaves:** open the person in Staff and tap **Has left**. Their PIN stops working at once on every phone. **Stop access** does the same without marking them as left.

**A phone you no longer use:** Settings > Your PIN > **Sign out of this phone**.

## Finding your way

- **On a phone:** the main pages are in the **tabs along the bottom**: Home, Day list, Sell, Orders and **More** (all the other pages, Production included). The round orange **+ button** adds things: a sale, an order, a batch, a purchase, an expense, spoiled juice, a fridge temperature or a reminder.
- **On a laptop:** the pages are listed down the left in three groups: **Daily work**, **Making juice** and **The business**. The orange **New** button does the same as the + button.
- Tap any row (an order, a batch, a customer, a purchase) to open it.
- To close a window, tap the **X** at its top right.
- At the bottom of the menu (or the top of the phone screen) the small cloud icon shows whether your changes have reached the cloud (**Saved**, **Sending**, or **Offline**: they will be sent when the connection returns). Next to it, your name with a lock: tap it to lock the app.

**Before anything is sent:** every WhatsApp message (receipts, delivery notes, the list of days, reminders) opens first in a preview: the message as it will look in the chat, who it goes to, and a box to change it. Nothing is sent until you tap **Send on WhatsApp**; WhatsApp then opens with it ready and you press its send button. **Copy** copies it instead. The statement card is shown the same way, as a picture, before **Send on WhatsApp**.

## Home

Home is the day at a glance:

- **Sold today**: bottles sold at the counter and orders delivered today, and **Money in today**, split into cash and mobile money.
- **Made today** (from Production or the Day list's bottles made), **Juice in the fridge** (and how many reach their best-before by tomorrow) and **Customers owe you**.
- **Needs attention**: today's reminders, fruit and packaging below the reorder level, juice to sell first, orders due by tomorrow that are not ready, orders to deliver today, a fridge that is too warm, or no fridge reading yet today. Each line has a button that takes you straight to it.
- **What the orders need by tomorrow**: each juice and size the orders need, what is in the fridge, and how many to make.

Staff who should not see money see counts instead of shillings.

## Day list

The day list is the Excel sheet in the app: each day, who took juice and who has paid.

**Writing the day:**

1. Open **Day list**. It opens on today; the arrows and the date go to another day.
2. Above the names, choose how a tap is paid: **Not yet** (on credit), **Cash**, **MTN MoMo** or **Airtel Money**.
3. **Tap a name** to add one bottle of the usual juice (shown above the names, e.g. Fresh juice 500 ml at UGX 3,000). The names are the customers who took juice most often; they stay in the same place all day.
4. A customer with **money paid ahead** (green button, with what is left) pays from it by itself.
5. For someone new, more bottles, another juice or another price (for example a 5,000 juice), tap **Someone else**. The juice and size chosen there become the usual one for the taps.
6. The day's names are listed below in the Excel colours. Tap one to record a payment or to undo it.

**Bottles made:** at the top of the day, type how many bottles you made (choose the juice and size if they differ) and tap **Add**. The three boxes then show **made**, **sold** and **left** (left now, or left that evening for a past day). Bottles left roll over to the next day and are sold first. If some went bad or were given away, tap **Some went bad or were given away**. Tap a count you added (e.g. "20 × Fresh juice 500 ml") to change it, delete it, or write off bottles from it. A juice with a recipe also takes its fruit off **Stock** for those litres.

**The week:** tap **Week** to see Monday to Friday side by side, like the sheet (Saturday and Sunday appear when juice was sold on them). On a phone, swipe sideways for Thursday and Friday. Tap a day to open it, or a name to see that sale. Under each day: made and left (once you count bottles made), sold and the money; below, the week's totals: days with juice, made, sold, spoiled, paid and not paid. Compare made with sold to see on which days you make too much or too little.

**The Excel colours in the app:**

| In the Excel sheet | In the app |
|---|---|
| Yellow day: juice supplied | Yellow day (worked out: someone took juice that day) |
| Red day: no juice | Red day (a weekday with nothing written; today stays plain until the day is over) |
| Blue name: paid | Blue name |
| No colour: not paid | White name in red letters; part paid is orange |
| Green side: paid in advance | Green name: paid from **money paid ahead** (see **Customers**) |
| "Apio-2", "Wasswa-5k" | Written the same way: 2 bottles, one 5,000 juice |

What the app adds to the sheet: the totals are always right, who owes what is on **Customers**, a customer who pays for several days is cleared oldest first, money paid ahead goes down by itself, and **Reports** show the month.

> **Records from the Excel sheet (14 September to 13 November 2026)** were put in as a juice called **Fresh juice** (3,000 = 500 ml; a 5,000 juice = 1 litre at 5,000), each marked "From the Excel sheet". The sheet does not say when the blue names paid, so they show as paid in cash on the day. The week of 21 to 25 September is not in the sheet, so those days show red.

## Sell

For sales at the counter.

1. The juices are in two groups: **Single fruit** and **Juice cocktails**. Each card shows the price of each size (and the bottles in the fridge, once batches are recorded).
2. Tap a size to add one bottle; tap again for more. (With **Sell only bottles recorded in the fridge** on in Settings, a size with none left is greyed out.)
3. On a laptop the sale builds up on the right (use **−** and **+** to change it). On a phone a dark bar at the bottom shows the bottles and the total.
4. Tap **Take payment**. Type the customer's name, or leave it blank for a walk-in. Choose how they paid: **Cash**, **MTN MoMo**, **Airtel Money**, **On credit**, or **From money paid ahead** (shown when the customer has some left). For cash, type what they gave you and the app shows the **change**. On credit needs the customer's name; the amount goes on their account.
5. Type how many **empty bottles** they brought back, if any.
6. Tap **Complete sale**. The receipt appears; **WhatsApp** sends it to the customer.

The bottles come off the batch with the **nearest best-before first**, so older juice goes out first. Bottles not recorded in a batch are still sold (unless Settings says otherwise). To undo a mistake, open the sale from **Today's sales** (beside the sale on a laptop, at the bottom of the page on a phone) and tap **Undo sale**: the bottles go back into the fridge.

## Orders and deliveries

For shops, schools, offices, canteens and events that order ahead.

**A new order:** tap **New order** (or **+**, then **New order**).

1. Type the customer's name. A new name is added to Customers; choose its type and phone.
2. Choose the delivery day (and time), the rider and where.
3. Under **What they want**, pick each juice, the size and how many. The price fills in by itself; change it for a special price. **Add a line** for more.
4. Add a delivery charge and any deposit paid now.
5. Under **Reminders**, the app has already ticked: remind production to **make it the day before at 6:00 am**, and remind the rider on the **delivery morning at 7:00 am**. Tick **Remind me to collect the balance** if needed.
6. Tap **Save order**.

**Following an order:** tap it. The bar at the top shows where it is: **To make**, **Ready**, **Out for delivery**, **Delivered**.

- **Mark ready** takes the bottles out of the fridge for the order (oldest first). With **Sell only bottles recorded in the fridge** on in Settings, the app refuses when the fridge has too few and says how many are there.
- **Mark out for delivery**, then **Mark delivered** when it arrives. A delivered order counts as sold that day.
- **Record payment** each time the customer pays. You cannot record more than the balance.
- **Delivery note** sends the order to the customer on WhatsApp. **Remind me** adds a reminder for this order.
- **Change** (while To make), **Back to To make** (puts packed bottles back), **Cancel order** (keeps the history and payments; bottles go back), **Delete** (removes it for good).

The chips at the top show **Open**, **To make**, **Ready**, **Out for delivery**, **Delivered**, **Unpaid** or **All**.

## Reminders

Reminders make sure nobody forgets: juice to make for a pre-order, a delivery, money to collect, fruit to buy, wages to pay, the fridge reading.

**Reminders that make themselves:** saving an order makes its reminders (see Orders). They go away by themselves when the order is delivered or cancelled.

**A reminder by hand:** tap **+**, then **Reminder** (or **New reminder** on the Reminders page, or **Remind me** on an order). Type what it is about, pick the day, the time, who it is for and whether it repeats (once, every day, every week). Then choose how it reaches the phone:

- **The phone's calendar:** the app gives a calendar file with an alarm 15 minutes before. Open it and tap **Add** (iPhone) or **Save** (Android). It then rings even with no internet or with the app closed.
- **WhatsApp message** to the person it is for (their phone number must be in Staff). WhatsApp opens with the message written; press **Send**.

The **Reminders** page lists what is coming up (overdue, today, tomorrow, later) and the repeating ones. On each: the **calendar** button adds it to the phone's calendar, the **WhatsApp** button sends it, and the **tick** marks it done. Tap a reminder to change it. Each person sees their own reminders; the owner sees everyone's.

> Notifications sent by the app itself (without the calendar) are planned for later.

## Production

Where each batch of juice is recorded.

- The tiles show the litres made and bottled this week, the juice cost per litre, and batches still being made.
- **What to make for tomorrow's orders** compares the orders with the fridge.
- Below is every batch (B-0001, B-0002...) with the juice, litres, bottles, best-before date and where it is: **Making**, **In the fridge**, **Best before today**, or **Finished**.

**A new batch:** tap **New batch**.

1. Pick the juice and type the **litres** made.
2. **What went in** fills in from the recipe. Change any amount if you used more or less. Each line says, in green, how much is in stock, or in red if there is not enough.
3. **Bottles filled**: the app suggests how many of each size; change them to what you really filled. If the juice is still being made, tick **Still being made: fill the bottles later**.
4. The app shows the **batch cost**, the **cost per litre**, what the bottles bring in if all are sold, and the **best-before** date (from the days the juice keeps).
5. Tick the **hygiene checks**, then **Save batch**. The fruit and bottles come off stock and the bottles go into the fridge.

Open a batch to see what went in, what was filled and what is left, its cost, and to **Fill the bottles** (for a batch still being made) or **Write off spoiled**.

## Recipes and prices

One card for each juice and juice cocktail. The chips show **All**, **Single fruit**, **Juice cocktails** or **Hidden**.

Each card shows the recipe for **10 litres**, how many days it keeps, and for each bottle size: the **cost**, the **price**, the **profit** and the **margin** (green when healthy, yellow when thin, red when too low). Costs use the **last price paid** for each fruit, so when mangoes get dearer every juice with mango shows it.

- **New cocktail** (or **New single-fruit juice**): give it a name, what is in it, a picture and colour, how many days it keeps, and normal or premium price. Then the recipe, one of two ways:
  - **What goes into 10 litres:** for each fruit or ingredient, pick it from the drop-down (the names already in Stock, so the same fruit is never written two ways), or choose **+ New ingredient…** and type its name and what it is counted in (fruit in pieces, sugar in kg). Type the amount for 10 litres, and the water. **Add a fruit or ingredient** for more.
  - **Work it out from a batch:** make a batch as usual and write down everything that went in (in total), the water, and the **bottles filled** of each size (300 ml, 500 ml, 1 litre, 5 litre). The app adds up the litres and works out the recipe for 10 litres, shown before you save. Leave **Also record this batch as made** ticked to count it on the Day list (bottles made) and take its fruit off Stock; change **Day it was made** for a batch made on an earlier day.
  - A new name that looks like one already in Stock (Mango and Mangoes) shows **Use Mangoes**, to keep one name. New ingredients are added to Stock when you save.
  - The cost and margin of each size show as you go (once fruit has been bought on Buying), so you can try mixes before you make them.
- **Reconcile bottles made** (on each juice, and at the top of the page; also **What went into these bottles?** on the Day list): for bottles already counted, e.g. this week's bottles made, with nothing recorded about what went in. Choose the juice and the days (**From** starts at the oldest bottles not yet reconciled, and can go back to any date). Tick the counts it lists, type everything that went into all of them together and the water, and save. The app shares it out over those bottles by litres (so Stock and the cost of those bottles are right) and, with **Make this the juice's recipe for 10 litres** ticked, works out the recipe from the same sums.
- **Change recipe** opens the same form. **Hide** takes a juice off Sell, the Day list, Production and new orders but keeps its history.
- **Delete** (the bin on each juice, or at the bottom of its form): a juice that has never been made, sold or ordered is deleted with its recipe. One that has (such as "Fresh juice" from the Excel sheet) can be **moved to another juice, then deleted**: every sale, order and batch becomes the other juice, amounts and payments unchanged. Or **Hide** it.

## Stock

Three tabs:

- **Juice in the fridge:** bottles of each juice and size, single fruit and cocktails apart, worked out from the batches. **Fridge temperatures today** (tap **Record** each morning; keep 0 to 5 °C). **By batch**: every batch in the fridge with its best-before; sell the oldest first. **Write off spoiled** records juice that went bad, broke or was given away.
- **Fruit and ingredients** and **Bottles and packaging:** what you have, with a bar that turns red below the reorder level. **Buy** records a purchase; **Count** corrects the stock after counting (rotten fruit thrown away, a miscount). Tap an item to change its name, unit or reorder level, or to hide it. **New fruit or ingredient** / **New packaging item** adds one.

**Fruit is counted in pieces** (mangoes, passion fruits, oranges, lemons...), so buy, count and write recipes in pieces; sugar stays in kg and mint in bunches. The usual set comes with typical counts per 10 litres (e.g. 30 mangoes); change them on **Recipes and prices** to what you really use. A new item starts in pieces; choose another unit in its form if needed.

Stock is always worked out: bought, less what batches used (fruit, and one bottle, cap and label for each bottle filled), plus or minus counts.

## Buying and suppliers

Every purchase of fruit, sugar and packaging.

**Buying:** tap **Buy**. Choose what was bought, type the supplier (a new name is added to Suppliers), the quantity (fruit in pieces) and the price per unit (per piece for fruit) (the total is worked out), what you paid now (leave 0 if on credit; **Paid in full** fills it) and, for fruit, the quality. Saving adds it to stock and updates the recipe costs.

Open a purchase to **Pay the supplier** or delete it. The page also shows what was bought this month, what you **owe suppliers**, and the price of your most-bought fruit over the last purchases, to follow the season.

## Customers

Everyone who buys on order, on credit or from the day list. The tiles at the top show how many customers bought in the last 4 weeks, what they owe you (and how many are **overdue**: a day unpaid for more than 7 days), the **collection rate** (how much of everything billed is paid) and the money paid ahead not used yet.

Search, sort (**Owes most**, **Collection rate**, **Billed**, **Bottles a week**, **Last active**, **Name**; the button next to it turns the order round), or use the chips: by type, **Owing**, **Overdue**, **Paid ahead**, **Hidden**.

**Cards or list:** the two small buttons on the right switch between **cards** and a **list** (each phone remembers the choice). A card shows, for one customer:

- how many times they bought and how many bottles a week (last 4 weeks), and a red **overdue** badge, or **Paid up** / **Paid ahead**;
- **Billed**, **Paid** and **Owes**, and any money paid ahead not used yet;
- the **collection rate** with a bar: green from 85%, orange from 60%, red below;
- the day they last bought and their oldest unpaid day;
- **Statement** and **Money received**; the arrow opens the customer.

Tap a customer to see what they owe, day by day, oldest first, and:

- **Money received:** type the amount once, even for many days. It pays what they owe, **oldest day first**; anything more is kept as **money paid ahead**. A customer who owes nothing and pays ahead has it all kept as paid ahead.
- **Pay from money paid ahead:** when they owe something and have money paid ahead, this uses it for what they owe (no new money comes in).
- **Money paid ahead** list: each amount paid ahead, and how much has gone on bottles. The bin removes one entered by mistake (not once bottles have used it).
- **Call**, **Send statement**, **New order**, **Change** their details or **Hide** them.

**The statement card:** **Statement** (on the card) or **Send statement** (in the customer) makes a picture in Vanny's colours, with the juice photo as a watermark, that tells the customer one thing: their balance (what they owe less anything paid ahead), or **All paid up**, or what they have **paid ahead** (and about how many bottles that is), as at today. No list of days. **Send on WhatsApp** opens the phone's share list: choose WhatsApp, then the customer. On a laptop the picture is saved and WhatsApp opens, to attach it. **Save picture** keeps it; **Send the list of days instead** sends the old text list.

Every bottle paid from money paid ahead (Day list, Sell or an order) takes it down by itself; undoing that sale puts the money back.

## Expenses

Running costs only: rent, power and water, gas, transport, repairs, cleaning, market dues. (Fruit and bottles are under Buying; wages under Staff, so nothing is counted twice.)

Tap **Expense**, type what it was for, the amount, date, group and how you paid. The page shows this month's total and each group's share. Tap an expense to change or delete it.

## Staff and wages

Add each person with **Person**: name, the **part of the app they use** (see below), their job, how they are paid (by the day, by the month, or no wage), the amount, and their phone (for WhatsApp reminders).

The squares are this week, Monday to Sunday. Tap a day once for **present** (green), again for **absent** (red), again to clear. For day workers the app works out the **wages due**: days present times the day rate, less advances and wages already paid this week. Monthly staff show what is due this month.

**Pay** records wages (the amount due is filled in). **Advance** records money given early; it comes off the next wages. **Has left** (on the person's form) hides someone but keeps their records, and stops their PIN at once.

Open a person to give them their **PIN** (their phone number and 6 digits), a **new PIN** if they forgot it, or to **stop access** (see **Opening the app**).

## Reports

Choose the period at the top: this month, last month, this year, last year, all time, or your own dates.

- **Profit**, worked out two ways (the two buttons on the Profit card; the app remembers your choice):
  - **By recipe:** bottles sold (counter sales on their day, orders on the day delivered) and delivery charges, less the juice and bottles **of what was sold**, running costs, wages and advances, and spoiled juice. Each bottle's juice costs what its recipe (or its batch) used, at the prices paid on **Buying**; fruit not yet used is not a cost until its juice is sold. This is the true profit once every juice has its recipe.
  - **By what was bought:** the same sales, less everything bought on **Buying** in the period (fruit and ingredients, bottles and packaging, used or not), running costs, and wages. Easy to check against the receipts, and it works before the recipes are right; a big purchase at the end of the month makes that month look lower and the next one higher.
  - Under the card, the other way's profit is shown too, so you can compare.
- **Fruit, ingredients and packaging bought:** what was spent on each item in the period (from Buying). These are kept apart from **running costs**, which are under Expenses.
- **Money in:** what customers paid, money paid ahead included on the day it came. A bottle paid from money paid ahead is a sale but not new money.
- **How customers paid** and **Where sales came from** (counter, and orders by type of customer).
- A yellow note says when a juice has **no fruit cost** in the records (no recipe, or its fruit never bought in the app): its profit then shows too high.
- **How each juice is doing:** as **cards** (the small buttons switch to a table). Each juice, best seller first: bottles, sales, profit, **share of sales**, margin, how many customers bought it, on how many days, spoiled bottles, when it last sold, and **up or down on the period before** (for this month: against last month). The table shows the same profit by juice, single fruit and juice cocktails apart. Use it to see which juices and mixes earn the most and which need a new price.
- **Running costs by group.**

**Print / PDF** prints the report (choose **Save as PDF** to keep a file). **Excel** downloads the profit by juice as a spreadsheet file.

## Settings

- **Your PIN:** lock the app now, change your PIN, or sign out of this phone (see **Opening the app**).
- **Business:** name, WhatsApp and phone numbers, the message at the bottom of receipts, mobile money merchant code.
- **Bottle sizes and prices:** the normal and premium price of each size, what the bottle, cap and label cost, which bottle from Stock each size uses up, which packaging **every bottle also uses** (caps, labels), and the deposit on a returnable bottle. New prices apply to new sales and orders only.
- **Lists and freshness:** ways of paying, expense groups, fridges, the highest fridge temperature, the hygiene checks, and **Sell only bottles recorded in the fridge** (off at first: you can sell before recording production; turn it on once every batch is recorded).
- **Backup:** **Download a backup** saves every record in one file; keep one every week somewhere safe (send it to yourself on WhatsApp, or save it to Drive). **Restore a backup** reads such a file back. **Delete all records** wipes everything on every phone (you must type DELETE), and stops every staff PIN. If records were saved on a phone before the app moved to the cloud, Backup shows **Add them to the cloud records**: tap it once on that phone.
