# Vanny's Fresh Juice user manual

Last updated: 12 November 2026 (first version of the app)

This app keeps the whole juice business in one place: the fruit you buy, the juice you make, what is in the fridge, what you sell and deliver, what customers owe, reminders, expenses, staff and profit. It works on a phone like an app and on a laptop in the browser.

> **For now the records are kept on the phone or laptop you use.** Connecting the app to the cloud, so every phone shares the same records and each person opens the app with their own PIN, is the next step. Until then, download a backup every week (Settings > Backup).

## Putting it on your phone

**iPhone**

1. Open the app's address in **Safari** (not Chrome).
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

**Android**

1. Open the app's address in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **three dots** at the top right.
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**), then **Add**.

The Vanny's icon now sits on your Home Screen and opens full screen like any other app, even with no signal.

> On a laptop, open the same address in Chrome or Edge and bookmark it.

## Getting started

The first time, Home shows **Getting started**:

1. Tap **Add the usual juices, fruit and bottles**. This adds 5 single-fruit juices and 7 juice cocktails with their recipes, the fruit and ingredients, and the bottles, caps and labels. Change any of them later on **Recipes and prices** and **Stock**.
2. Tap **Add the staff** and add everyone who works at Vanny's, the owner too (see **Staff and wages**).
3. Check the bottle sizes and prices in **Settings**.
4. Record what you buy (**Buying**), then each batch you make (**Production**). The fridge fills up, and you can sell.

## Finding your way

- **On a phone:** the main pages are in the **tabs along the bottom**: Home, Sell, Orders, Make and **More** (all the other pages). The round orange **+ button** adds things: a sale, an order, a batch, a purchase, an expense, spoiled juice, a fridge temperature or a reminder.
- **On a laptop:** the pages are listed down the left in three groups: **Daily work**, **Making juice** and **The business**. The orange **New** button does the same as the + button.
- Tap any row (an order, a batch, a customer, a purchase) to open it.
- To close a window, tap the **X** at its top right.
- At the bottom of the menu (or the top of the phone screen) the small icon shows where records are kept: **Kept on this device** for now.

## Home

Home is the day at a glance:

- **Sold today**: bottles sold at the counter and orders delivered today, and **Money in today**, split into cash and mobile money.
- **Made today**, **Juice in the fridge** (and how many reach their best-before by tomorrow) and **Customers owe you**.
- **Needs attention**: today's reminders, fruit and packaging below the reorder level, juice to sell first, orders due by tomorrow that are not ready, orders to deliver today, a fridge that is too warm, or no fridge reading yet today. Each line has a button that takes you straight to it.
- **What the orders need by tomorrow**: each juice and size the orders need, what is in the fridge, and how many to make.

Staff who should not see money see counts instead of shillings.

## Sell

For sales at the counter.

1. The juices are in two groups: **Single fruit** and **Juice cocktails**. Each card shows the price of each size and how many bottles are left in the fridge.
2. Tap a size to add one bottle; tap again for more. A size with none left is greyed out.
3. On a laptop the sale builds up on the right (use **−** and **+** to change it). On a phone a dark bar at the bottom shows the bottles and the total.
4. Tap **Take payment**. Type the customer's name, or leave it blank for a walk-in. Choose how they paid: **Cash**, **MTN MoMo**, **Airtel Money** or **On credit**. For cash, type what they gave you and the app shows the **change**. On credit needs the customer's name; the amount goes on their account.
5. Type how many **empty bottles** they brought back, if any.
6. Tap **Complete sale**. The receipt appears; **WhatsApp** sends it to the customer.

The bottles come off the batch with the **nearest best-before first**, so older juice goes out first. To undo a mistake, open the sale from **Today's sales** (beside the sale on a laptop, at the bottom of the page on a phone) and tap **Undo sale**: the bottles go back into the fridge.

## Orders and deliveries

For shops, schools, offices, canteens and events that order ahead.

**A new order:** tap **New order** (or **+**, then **New order**).

1. Type the customer's name. A new name is added to Customers; choose its type and phone.
2. Choose the delivery day (and time), the rider and where.
3. Under **What they want**, pick each juice, the size and how many. The price fills in by itself; change it for a special price. **Add a line** for more.
4. Add a delivery charge and any deposit paid now.
5. Under **Reminders**, the app has already ticked: remind production to **make it the day before at 6:00 am**, and remind the rider on the **delivery morning at 7:00 am**. Tick **Remind me to collect the balance** if needed.
6. Tap **Save order**.

**Following an order:** tap it. The bar at the top shows where it is: **To make**, **Ready**, **Out for delivery**, **Delivered**.

- **Mark ready** takes the bottles out of the fridge for the order (oldest first). If the fridge has too few, the app says how many are there: make a batch first.
- **Mark out for delivery**, then **Mark delivered** when it arrives. A delivered order counts as sold that day.
- **Record payment** each time the customer pays. You cannot record more than the balance.
- **Delivery note** sends the order to the customer on WhatsApp. **Remind me** adds a reminder for this order.
- **Change** (while To make), **Back to To make** (puts packed bottles back), **Cancel order** (keeps the history and payments; bottles go back), **Delete** (removes it for good).

The chips at the top show **Open**, **To make**, **Ready**, **Out for delivery**, **Delivered**, **Unpaid** or **All**.

## Reminders

Reminders make sure nobody forgets: juice to make for a pre-order, a delivery, money to collect, fruit to buy, wages to pay, the fridge reading.

**Reminders that make themselves:** saving an order makes its reminders (see Orders). They go away by themselves when the order is delivered or cancelled.

**A reminder by hand:** tap **+**, then **Reminder** (or **New reminder** on the Reminders page, or **Remind me** on an order). Type what it is about, pick the day, the time, who it is for and whether it repeats (once, every day, every week). Then choose how it reaches the phone:

- **The phone's calendar:** the app gives a calendar file with an alarm 15 minutes before. Open it and tap **Add** (iPhone) or **Save** (Android). It then rings even with no internet or with the app closed.
- **WhatsApp message** to the person it is for (their phone number must be in Staff). WhatsApp opens with the message written; press **Send**.

The **Reminders** page lists what is coming up (overdue, today, tomorrow, later) and the repeating ones. On each: the **calendar** button adds it to the phone's calendar, the **WhatsApp** button sends it, and the **tick** marks it done. Tap a reminder to change it. Each person sees their own reminders; the owner sees everyone's.

> Notifications sent by the app itself (without the calendar) are planned for later.

## Production

Where each batch of juice is recorded.

- The tiles show the litres made and bottled this week, the juice cost per litre, and batches still being made.
- **What to make for tomorrow's orders** compares the orders with the fridge.
- Below is every batch (B-0001, B-0002...) with the juice, litres, bottles, best-before date and where it is: **Making**, **In the fridge**, **Best before today**, or **Finished**.

**A new batch:** tap **New batch**.

1. Pick the juice and type the **litres** made.
2. **What went in** fills in from the recipe. Change any amount if you used more or less. Each line says, in green, how much is in stock, or in red if there is not enough.
3. **Bottles filled**: the app suggests how many of each size; change them to what you really filled. If the juice is still being made, tick **Still being made: fill the bottles later**.
4. The app shows the **batch cost**, the **cost per litre**, what the bottles bring in if all are sold, and the **best-before** date (from the days the juice keeps).
5. Tick the **hygiene checks**, then **Save batch**. The fruit and bottles come off stock and the bottles go into the fridge.

Open a batch to see what went in, what was filled and what is left, its cost, and to **Fill the bottles** (for a batch still being made) or **Write off spoiled**.

## Recipes and prices

One card for each juice and juice cocktail. The chips show **All**, **Single fruit**, **Juice cocktails** or **Hidden**.

Each card shows the recipe for **10 litres**, how many days it keeps, and for each bottle size: the **cost**, the **price**, the **profit** and the **margin** (green when healthy, yellow when thin, red when too low). Costs use the **last price paid** for each fruit, so when mangoes get dearer every juice with mango shows it.

- **New cocktail** (or **New single-fruit juice**): give it a name, what is in it, a picture and colour, then each fruit and how much goes into 10 litres (**Add a fruit** for more), the water, how many days it keeps, and normal or premium price. The cost and margin of each size show as you type, so you can try mixes before you make them.
- **Change recipe** opens the same form. **Hide** takes a juice off Sell, Production and new orders but keeps its history.

## Stock

Three tabs:

- **Juice in the fridge:** bottles of each juice and size, single fruit and cocktails apart, worked out from the batches. **Fridge temperatures today** (tap **Record** each morning; keep 0 to 5 °C). **By batch**: every batch in the fridge with its best-before; sell the oldest first. **Write off spoiled** records juice that went bad, broke or was given away.
- **Fruit and ingredients** and **Bottles and packaging:** what you have, with a bar that turns red below the reorder level. **Buy** records a purchase; **Count** corrects the stock after counting (rotten fruit thrown away, a miscount). Tap an item to change its name, unit or reorder level, or to hide it. **New fruit or ingredient** / **New packaging item** adds one.

Stock is always worked out: bought, less what batches used (fruit, and one bottle, cap and label for each bottle filled), plus or minus counts.

## Buying and suppliers

Every purchase of fruit, sugar and packaging.

**Buying:** tap **Buy**. Choose what was bought, type the supplier (a new name is added to Suppliers), the quantity and the price per unit (the total is worked out), what you paid now (leave 0 if on credit; **Paid in full** fills it) and, for fruit, the quality. Saving adds it to stock and updates the recipe costs.

Open a purchase to **Pay the supplier** or delete it. The page also shows what was bought this month, what you **owe suppliers**, and the price of your most-bought fruit over the last purchases, to follow the season.

## Customers

Everyone who buys on order or on credit. Search, or use the chips: by type, **Owing**, **Hidden**. Each shows what they **owe**. Tap one to see their orders and credit sales, **Call**, **Send statement** on WhatsApp, start a **New order**, **Change** their details or **Hide** them.

## Expenses

Running costs only: rent, power and water, gas, transport, repairs, cleaning, market dues. (Fruit and bottles are under Buying; wages under Staff, so nothing is counted twice.)

Tap **Expense**, type what it was for, the amount, date, group and how you paid. The page shows this month's total and each group's share. Tap an expense to change or delete it.

## Staff and wages

Add each person with **Person**: name, the **part of the app they use** (see below), their job, how they are paid (by the day, by the month, or no wage), the amount, and their phone (for WhatsApp reminders).

The squares are this week, Monday to Sunday. Tap a day once for **present** (green), again for **absent** (red), again to clear. For day workers the app works out the **wages due**: days present times the day rate, less advances and wages already paid this week. Monthly staff show what is due this month.

**Pay** records wages (the amount due is filled in). **Advance** records money given early; it comes off the next wages. **Has left** (on the person's form) hides someone but keeps their records.

## Reports

Choose the period at the top: this month, last month, this year, last year, all time, or your own dates.

- **Profit:** bottles sold (counter sales on their day, orders on the day delivered) and delivery charges, less the juice and bottles of what was sold, running costs, wages and advances, and spoiled juice.
- **How customers paid** and **Where sales came from** (counter, and orders by type of customer).
- **Profit by juice:** single fruit and juice cocktails apart, each with its share of sales, so you can see which juices and mixes earn the most and which need a new price.
- **Running costs by group.**

**Print / PDF** prints the report (choose **Save as PDF** to keep a file). **Excel** downloads the profit by juice as a spreadsheet file.

## Settings

- **Business:** name, WhatsApp and phone numbers, the message at the bottom of receipts, mobile money merchant code.
- **Bottle sizes and prices:** the normal and premium price of each size, what the bottle, cap and label cost, which bottle from Stock each size uses up, which packaging **every bottle also uses** (caps, labels), and the deposit on a returnable bottle. New prices apply to new sales and orders only.
- **Lists and freshness:** ways of paying, expense groups, fridges, the highest fridge temperature, and the hygiene checks.
- **Backup:** **Download a backup** saves every record in one file; keep one every week somewhere safe (send it to yourself on WhatsApp, or save it to Drive). **Restore a backup** reads such a file back. **Delete all records** wipes everything (you must type DELETE).

## People and what they can open

| Part of the app | Opens |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything, including profit, wages and Settings |
| Production | Home, Production, Recipes, Stock, Buying |
| Sales (counter) | Home, Sell, Orders, Customers, Stock |
| Rider | Home and Orders (the balance to collect, no other money) |

Everyone also has **Reminders** (their own) and **Help**.

For now, on a shared phone, tap the name chip at the top (or at the bottom of the menu on a laptop) and choose **who is using the app**. When the app is connected to the cloud, each person will open it with their own PIN instead.
