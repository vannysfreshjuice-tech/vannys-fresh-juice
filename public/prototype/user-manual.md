# Vanny's Fresh Juice user manual

Last updated: 12 November 2026 (first version, for the prototype)

This app keeps the whole juice business in one place: the fruit you buy, the juice you make, what is in the fridge, what you sell and deliver, what customers owe, expenses, staff and profit. It works on a phone like an app and on a laptop in the browser.

> **This is a prototype.** The figures are made up and nothing you type is saved. It is here so you can see how the app will look and work, and tell us what to change. This manual will be kept up to date as the real app is built.

## Putting it on your phone

**iPhone**

1. Open the app's address in **Safari** (not Chrome).
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

**Android**

1. Open the app's address in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **three dots** at the top right.
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**), then **Add**.

The Vanny's icon now sits on your Home Screen and opens full screen like any other app.

> On a laptop, open the same address in Chrome or Edge and bookmark it.

## Opening the app

The app opens on the **PIN** screen. Type your 6-digit PIN on the number pad (on a laptop the number keys work too). Each person has their own PIN, and the app shows only the parts that person needs (see **People and what they can open**).

*In the prototype, any 6 digits open it.*

## Finding your way

- **On a phone:** the main pages are in the **tabs along the bottom**: Home, Sell, Orders, Make and **More** (all the other pages). The round orange **+ button** adds things: a sale, an order, a batch, a purchase, an expense, or spoiled juice to write off.
- **On a laptop:** all pages are listed down the left in three groups: **Daily work**, **Making juice** and **The business**. The orange **New** button at the top does the same as the + button.
- Tap any row with an arrow or a name (an order, a batch, a customer) to open it.
- To close a window, tap the **X** at its top right or tap outside it.
- **Help (user manual)**, this manual, is at the bottom of the list on the left, or under **More** on a phone. It opens with its **Contents**: tap a chapter to jump to it.

## Home

Home is the day at a glance:

- **Sold today** and **Money in today**, split into cash and mobile money.
- **Made today**: litres made and how many batches.
- **Juice in the fridge**: bottles ready to sell, and how many reach their best-before tomorrow.
- **Customers owe you**: the total, and how many customers.
- **Needs attention**: fruit running low, juice to sell first, bottles to reorder, orders not yet made, a fridge that is too warm. Each line has a button that takes you straight to the fix (**Buy**, **See**, **Plan**).
- **Today's production plan**, **Sales this week** and **Best sellers today**.

Staff who should not see money (production, counter, rider) see counts instead of shillings.

## Sell

For sales at the counter.

1. The juices are in two groups: **Single fruit** and **Juice cocktails**. Each card shows the price and how many bottles of each size are in the fridge.
2. Tap a size to add one bottle. Tap again for more. A size with none left is greyed out.
3. On a laptop the sale builds up on the right. On a phone a dark bar at the bottom shows the bottles and the total.
4. Tap **Take payment**. Choose the **Customer** (Walk-in for counter sales) and how they paid: **Cash**, **MTN MoMo**, **Airtel Money** or **On credit**.
   - For cash, type what they gave you and the app shows the **change**.
   - For mobile money you can type the transaction ID.
   - On credit puts the amount on the customer's account.
5. Tick **Customer returned empty bottles** if they brought bottles back.
6. Tap **Complete sale**. The receipt appears; **Send on WhatsApp** sends it to the customer.

The bottles come off the **oldest batch first**, so juice near its best-before goes out first.

## Orders and deliveries

For shops, schools, offices, canteens and events that order ahead.

**A new order:** tap **New order** (or **+**, then **New order**). Choose the customer, the delivery day and the rider, then each juice, size and how many. Add a delivery charge and any deposit paid now, then **Save order**.

**Following an order:** tap it to open it. The bar at the top shows where it is: **To make**, **Ready**, **Out for delivery**, **Delivered**. Tap **Mark ready** (and so on) to move it along.

- **Record payment** each time the customer pays. The balance is worked out for you. You cannot record more than the balance.
- **Delivery note** sends the order to the customer on WhatsApp.
- The chips at the top (**To make**, **Ready**, **Unpaid**...) show only those orders.

## Reminders

Reminders make sure nobody forgets: juice to make for a pre-order, a delivery, money to collect, fruit to buy, wages to pay.

**Reminders that make themselves:** when you save an order, the app offers (already ticked) to remind the production person to **make it the day before at 6:00 am** and the rider on the **delivery morning at 7:00 am**. You can also tick **Remind me to collect the balance**.

**A reminder by hand:** tap **+**, then **Reminder** (or **New reminder** on the Reminders page, or **Remind me** on an open order). Type what it is about, pick the day, the time, who it is for and whether it repeats (once, every day, every week). Then choose how it should reach the phone:

- **Notification from the app:** the phone shows it at that time, like a WhatsApp message. On an iPhone the app must be on the Home Screen, and the first time you must tap **Allow** when it asks about notifications. **Send a test notification** on the Reminders page checks that it works.
- **Add to the phone's calendar:** the app makes a calendar entry with an alarm 15 minutes before. Open it and tap **Add** (iPhone) or **Save** (Android). It then rings even with no internet or with the app closed.
- **WhatsApp message to the person:** handy for staff, so the rider gets the delivery reminder on his own phone.

The **Reminders** page lists what is coming up (overdue, today, tomorrow, later) and the repeating ones. Tap the **calendar** button to put any reminder in the calendar, and the **tick** when it is done. Home shows today's reminders at the top of **Needs attention**. Each person sees only their own reminders; the owner sees everyone's.

## Production

Where each batch of juice is recorded.

- **What to make for tomorrow** compares what tomorrow's orders need with what is in the fridge, and warns when fruit is short.
- **Hygiene before each batch** is ticked by whoever makes the batch: hands washed, fruit washed, equipment sanitised, bottles rinsed, fridge cold.
- **Batches** lists every batch (B-0001, B-0002...) with the juice, litres, bottles, cost per litre, best-before date and where it is (**Making**, **In the fridge**, **Sold out**, or spoiled).

**A new batch:** tap **New batch**.

1. Pick the juice.
2. Type the **litres** you are making. The app suggests how many bottles of each size; change them if you fill differently.
3. Underneath, the app shows the **fruit, sugar and bottles needed**, in green when you have enough and in red when you do not, the **cost of the batch**, the **cost per litre** and the **best-before** date.
4. Tap **Save batch**. The fruit and bottles come off stock and the bottles go into the fridge.

Tap a batch to see what went into it, what was filled, what it cost and what it would sell for.

## Recipes and prices

One card for each juice and juice cocktail. The chips at the top show **All**, **Single fruit** or **Juice cocktails**.

Each card shows the recipe for **10 litres** (fruit, sugar, water), how many days it keeps, and for each bottle size: the **cost**, the **price**, the **profit** and the **margin**. Margins are green when healthy, yellow when thin and red when too low.

The costs use the prices from your **latest fruit purchases**, so when mangoes get dearer every juice with mango shows it straight away.

**A new juice cocktail:** tap **New cocktail**. Give it a name, then pick each fruit and how much goes into 10 litres (**Add a fruit** for more). The cost per litre and the margin for each bottle size appear as you type, so you can try mixes before you make them. Tap **Save cocktail** to add it to Sell, Production, Stock and Reports.

## Stock

Three tabs:

- **Juice in the fridge:** bottles of each juice and size, single fruit and cocktails apart. Below, every batch in the fridge with the days left (red: best before tomorrow). **Write off spoiled** records juice that went bad, broke or was given away. Also here: **fridge temperatures** (keep 0 to 5 °C; **Record a reading** each morning) and **empty bottles** out with customers.
- **Fruit and ingredients** and **Bottles and packaging:** what you have, the level at which to reorder, and what it is worth. Low items are red and have a **Buy** button.

The bottles in the fridge are worked out from the batches (filled, less sold, spoiled or given away), never typed in.

## Buying and suppliers

Every purchase of fruit, sugar, bottles, caps and labels.

**Buying:** tap **Buy** (or **+**, then **Buy fruit or supplies**). Choose the item and supplier, type the quantity and price per unit (the total is worked out), what you paid now (leave blank if on credit) and the quality of the fruit. Saving adds it to stock and updates the recipe costs.

The page also shows what you **owe each supplier** and the **price of mangoes week by week**, to follow the season.

## Customers

All your regular customers, by type (shop, school, office, events, canteen). Each shows what they **owe** and how many **empty bottles** they hold. Tap one to see their orders, **Send statement** on WhatsApp, or start a **New order**.

## Expenses

Running costs only: rent, power and water, gas, transport, repairs, cleaning, market dues. (Fruit and bottles are under Buying; wages under Staff.)

Tap **Expense**, type what it was for, the amount, date, group and how you paid. You can add a **photo of the receipt**. The page shows the month's total and each group's share.

## Staff and wages

Each worker with their role, pay (by the day or by the month) and any advance. The coloured squares are this week's attendance, Monday to Saturday: green present, red absent. For daily workers the app works out the **wages due**: days worked times the day rate, less advances.

**Mark today's attendance** each day and **Pay wages** on pay day.

## Reports

Choose the period at the top: **Today**, **This week**, **This month**, **Last month** or **Pick dates**.

- **Profit:** sales, less fruit and bottles used, running costs, wages and spoiled juice.
- **How customers paid** and **Where sales came from**.
- **Profit by flavour:** single fruit and juice cocktails apart, each with its share of sales, so you can see which juices and mixes earn the most and which need a new price.

**PDF** makes a report to print or share; **Excel** downloads the figures.

## Settings

- **Business:** name, WhatsApp and phone numbers, receipt message, mobile money merchant code.
- **Bottle sizes and prices:** the normal and premium price of each size and what the bottle, cap and label cost. The deposit on a returnable bottle.
- **People and what they can open:** each person's PIN and pages.
- **Freshness rules:** how many days each juice keeps, and the fridge temperature range.
- **Back up all records:** saves a copy of everything to a file. Do this every week.

## People and what they can open

| Person | Sees |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything, including profit and wages |
| Production | Home, Production, Recipes, Stock, Buying |
| Sales (counter) | Home, Sell, Orders, Customers, Stock |
| Rider | Home and Orders (no money) |

Everyone also has **Reminders** (their own) and **Help**.

*In the prototype, the **Viewing as** box at the top switches between them so you can see each person's app.*
