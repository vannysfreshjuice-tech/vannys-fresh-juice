# Vanny's Fresh Juice app

Business app for **Vanny's Fresh Juice** (a juice maker in Kampala; the owner is Vanessa), built by Denis Mwanje (MDM) with Claude Code from 12 Nov 2026. It covers the whole business: buying fruit and packaging, making juice in batches, the fridge, counter sales, orders and deliveries, reminders, customers, expenses, staff and wages, reports. Phone first (iPhone or Android not yet known), also works on a laptop.

Built from the lessons of MANSOL Finance (`D:\COMPANY-MANSOL\My kamoney Application`) and Bake Away (`D:\COMPANY-MANSOL\Bake away`, read its CLAUDE.md). The look-and-feel prototype that came first is kept in `public/prototype/` (served at `/prototype/`); the app itself is the Vite project at the root.

## Rules that must not be broken

1. **Figures are worked out, never stored.** Bottles in the fridge, stock on hand, cost per litre, paid, balance, pay status, wages due, profit: all come from the pure functions in `src/lib/juice.ts`, `src/lib/staff.ts` and `src/lib/reports.ts`, computed once in `src/data/DataContext.tsx`. Never add a stored `onHand`, `left`, `paid`, `balance` or `status` field.
2. **Every save goes through `src/data/actions.ts`.** New record = whole (`set`); edit = only its form's fields (`patch`). Things that belong together are one `store.commit([...])`: a sale with its payment, an order with its deposit and reminders, a delete with its payments.
3. **Bottles leave the fridge only in actions, oldest best-before first** (`takeFromFridge`): a counter sale records each line's `batchId`; an order takes its bottles when marked Ready (stored as `packed`), and gives them back when moved to To make or Cancelled. A batch with bottles gone cannot be deleted.
4. **Amounts cleaned once** in the action (`cleanAmount`, `cleanQty`), read with `num()`. Payments above the balance, blank or negative amounts are refused with a plain message.
5. **Forms cannot save twice** (`useSaver`), dates are local `YYYY-MM-DD` (`todayYmd()`, never `toISOString()`), settings store only changed fields (`withDefaults` fills the rest in memory), a failed load is not "no records" (`ready`, `LoadNotice`).
6. **A backup holds every collection** (from `COLLECTIONS` in `src/lib/types.ts`); a new collection goes into `COLLECTIONS` and `Records` and the backup covers it. `tests/saving.test.ts` proves the round trip.
7. **Records others point to are hidden, not deleted** (customers with orders, items in recipes, juices with batches).
8. **Phone first:** 44px touch targets, 16px field text, bottom sheets, no hover-only controls, no `alert()`/`confirm()`. Windows open through `useSheets().open({ t: ... })`; going to another page closes them.
9. **Plain words**, one name for one thing: Sale, Order, Batch, Purchase, Payment, Balance, Write off, Reminder.
10. **Who sees what** is defined once in `src/lib/nav.ts` (`PAGES[].roles`). Roles: owner, production, sales, rider. **Sign-in (Denis, 12 Nov 2026: "PIN, records shared"):** in the published app each person has their **own Firebase sign-in and their PIN is its password** (Firebase checks it; never a browser-only PIN with cloud records). Owner = the sign-in with `ownerEmail` (`src/firebase-config.ts`, the same address is written in `firestore.rules`). Staff sign-ins are made by the owner (Staff > person > Give a PIN: `giveStaffPin` in `src/data/cloud.ts`, on a second Firebase app so she stays signed in), with a made-up email under `staffDomain` (Google-owned, no mailboxes), found from their phone number through `logins/{256...}`. `members/{uid}` = { staffId, name } is what lets a staff sign-in in; only the owner writes it; "Has left", "Stop access" and "Delete all records" remove it in the same change (`accessRemoval` in `src/data/store.ts`). The role shown comes from the staff record the member points to (a staff sign-in never gets the owner's part); the rules enforce the sensitive part: only the owner changes staff, wages, settings and access, and only the owner reads wages (`OWNER_ONLY_READ` in cloud.ts skips that list for others). `src/components/CloudGate.tsx`: "Who is opening the app?" (owner PIN / owner first-time set-up / staff phone + PIN), then a PIN pad at start and after 5 min away, checked on the phone against a PBKDF2 fingerprint after Firebase accepted it once (`rememberCloudPin`, `vannys.cloudPin`), "Someone else" signs out. **Device mode** (`npm run dev`, records in localStorage): the PIN lock is off by default, set up in Settings, one fingerprint per person in `vannys.pins` (`LockGate` in `src/components/Lock.tsx`); without a PIN the person is picked with `WhoSheet`.
11. **No secrets in the repo.** The repo is public (GitHub Pages on a free account).
12. **Prove before handing over:** `npm run check`, `npm test`, `npm run build`, then the affected pages in the built-in browser at phone size (375 x 812) and laptop size, figures checked by hand.
13. **Keep the manual current:** `docs/user-manual.md` is the one copy, shown at Help (`src/pages/Manual.tsx`). Every change the user can see updates its chapter, "What's new" and "Last updated" in the same change.

- **Collections:** products (juices and cocktails: kind single | cocktail, recipe per 10 L, water, keepsDays, premium), items (stock: kind fruit | packaging), suppliers, purchases (one item each), counts (stock corrections), batches (used, filled by size, bestBefore), sales (counter, lines with batchId, method or 'On credit'), orders (stages To make, Ready, Out for delivery, Delivered, Cancelled; packed, deliveredOn), payments (kind order | sale | purchase | advance: money a customer paid ahead, refId = the customer; a sale or order paid from it has method `FROM_ADVANCE` 'From money paid ahead'), customers, writeOffs, expenses, staff (role, payKind day | month | none), attendance (id `date_staffId`), wages (wage | advance), reminders (date, time, staffId, orderId, repeat, ways calendar | whatsapp, done), readings (fridge temperatures), settings (`main`: sizes with price, premiumPrice, packCost, bottleItemId; perBottle items such as caps and labels; methods, expense groups, fridges, fridgeMax, hygiene checks, bottle deposit, fridgeCheck).

Report: sold = counter sales by date + orders by `deliveredOn`; profit = sales + delivery fees - cost of bottles sold - expenses - wages and advances - spoiled. Reports also offer **profit by what was bought** (Denis, 16 Nov 2026): sales + fees - everything bought on Buying in the period (fruit and packaging, used or not) - expenses - wages (`report.bought`); the choice is kept in `vannys.profitBasis`.
